Career

Career path

From a JNU doctorate to founding a think tank.

  1. 2002–03

    Visiting graduate student at the Rothberg International School, Hebrew University of Jerusalem, on an Israeli Foreign Ministry fellowship.

  2. 2006–07

    PhD at JNU on US–Israel strategic relations after the Cold War, then post-doctoral fellow at the Begin-Sadat Center, Bar-Ilan University.

  3. 2008–10

    Faculty at NALSAR University of Law, Hyderabad.

  4. 2010

    Research Associate at Vivekananda International Foundation on India’s counter-terrorism policy. Completed the counter-terrorism certificate at the International Institute for Counter-Terrorism, Herzliya.

  5. 2011–12

    US State Department IVLP fellowship to build an American Studies curriculum for Indian universities. Faculty at the Advanced Center for American Studies, Osmania University.

  6. 2012–13

    Visiting Research Fellow at the University of Utah, lecturing at the Hinckley Institute of Politics. Tanner Humanities Center fellow in 2013.

  7. Present
    • Center for Human Security Studies (CHSS). Founder and Executive Director of this independent Hyderabad think tank on national and human security, since March 2013. CHSS website
    • University Grants Commission (UGC). Chairman of the National Security Studies Experts Committee since March 2025, designing a SWAYAM MOOC course aligned with the National Education Policy 2020. The Hindu
    • AI Human Security Research Foundation. Founder Director since June 2025. LinkedIn
    • Telangana Council of Higher Education (TGCHE). Academic Consultant since November 2024.
    • IIT Bhubaneswar. Adjunct Faculty, teaching National Security.

AIHSRF

AIHS: Artificial Intelligence for Human Security Research Foundation

The CHSS Centre of Excellence studies what AI means for people’s safety, freedom and security, and works as a watchdog against its misuse.

  • Research on how AI is adopted and what it does to human security.
  • Analysis and reports on what AI means for human security.
  • A common platform for technology experts, think tanks, universities, companies and policy makers.
  • Debate on AI, IoT and blockchain and their effect on national security.
  • Conferences, seminars and workshops, national and international.
  • An AI talent pool built through research internships.
Society is at a turning point in deciding how AI can strengthen freedom, equality and transparency instead of weakening them.CHSS, AIHS

Malicious use of AI: a threat to human security

Cyber weapons

AI can build cyber weapons, impersonate targets and attack drones, smart cars or weapons systems with devastating effect.

Smarter hacking

Bots can find certain software bugs much faster than people, so attacks become more sophisticated.

Misinformation

AI can spread propaganda and false information, harming businesses as much as politics.
